Multi-Tenant Architecture

Each cafe tenant gets a UUID. All tenant-scoped collections are prefixed <uuid>_<collection> (e.g. 550e8400_e29b_staff).
The tenants collection is global; everything else lives under the tenant's namespace. Logical references between collections use plain UUID fields, resolved via $lookup aggregations or application-level joins.
